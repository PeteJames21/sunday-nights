Pretty as she is, perfection in her.
This is how she praised her to the masses.
Her precious gem, glowing.
However after the pressure needed to get molded.

They tell her to go, she does.
Stay, she stays as told. 
Give that to me, and she's resigned to do just that.
No mind of her own. 

What if she takes the lead for once.
They will take her down.
So she cowers, but
are they going to be there always...
