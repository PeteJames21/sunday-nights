---
title: What About Me
author: Zoya Snawer
date: 2026-09-23
---
Everyone wants something, and they get it .
But what do they give her? 
She's the generous one,the giver,
What about what she wants?

No one ever asks what they want.
Everyone one is seeking for themselves.
She feels choked, suffocated by ghosts.
Life, friends, family, their feelings—all at her neck.

But she can't kick the stool away.
They value their life, they look at the skies.
Questions in mind, life is valuable.
A purpose to accomplish—why, why.
