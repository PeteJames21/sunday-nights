---
title: I dreamt
author: Lihanda
date: 2026-06-24
---
I had a dream last night—
so vivid, so warm.

I dreamt I found you. 😊

In this life,
not the next one...
not in another dimension,
but this one.

Your hugs—so warm.
Your arms enveloped me.
I was home, truly.

You held my hands, refusing to let go.

"I choose her,"

were the words you said aloud.

My heart soared.

No, really—

I felt it jump out of my chest.

Unrequited love...

is who you became to me.

But then I woke up.

The dull ache I know all too well settled in my chest.

Your arms were gone.

And so I will seek you in my dreams,

for that is where I will always find you.
